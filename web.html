Facebook & Instagram

            
            
            

            
How to connect (about 10 minutes, once)
              
    
                
  1. For Instagram: make it a professional account (Business or Creator) and link it to your Facebook Page.
    2. 
                
  2. Create a Meta app ↗ (type Business). As its admin you can use it with your own Page in development mode.
    3. 
                
  3. In the Graph API Explorer ↗, pick your app and add the permissions pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish. Click Generate Access Token and allow your Page.
    4. 
                
  4. Paste that token into the Access Token Debugger ↗, click Extend Access Token, and copy the long-lived token.
    5. 
                
  5. Paste it below. The studio finds your Page and its Instagram account and keeps a Page token that doesn't expire.
    6. 
              

            

              

          

          

            
TikTok

            
            

            
How to connect (about 10 minutes, once)
              
    
                
  1. On TikTok for Developers ↗, create an app with the Desktop platform.
    2. 
                
  2. Add the products Login Kit and Content Posting API, with the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload.
    3. 
                
  3. Under Login Kit, add the redirect URI http://127.0.0.1:8766/callback/ exactly.
    4. 
                
  4. Use the app's Sandbox and add your TikTok account as a target user, or submit the app for review.
    5. 
                
  5. Paste the Client key and Client secret below, then click Connect TikTok.
    6. 
              

              
Posting straight to your profile needs TikTok to audit the app; until then TikTok only allows private posts on a private account, so the studio sends videos to your inbox instead.
